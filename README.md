# Larm

Larm — a UTC-only alarm &amp; timer app for gaming groups across timezones. Every alarm fires at its UTC instant and the dashboard shows only UTC time, so there's no ambiguity when travelling or coordinating across regions. Set custom alarms/timers, see live countdowns, and get full-screen alerts that respect your phone's sound settings. A single Aurora 2.0 dark glass theme layers drifting violet, cyan, mint, pink and amber light behind frosted panels, gradient neon digits and clear Lucide icons. No login required — just open and go.

## Highlights

- **UTC only** — no device-local clock or local alarm mode; the dashboard, editor preview, and ringing screen all show UTC.
- **Aurora 2.0 glass** — one animated dark theme: drifting neon light, gradient glass edges, glowing UTC clock and neon controls.
- **22 bundled tones** — 12 original classics plus 10 royalty-free "Popular" tones synthesized locally (`dart run tools/generate_sounds.dart`).
- **Live timers and full-screen alerts** — alarms and timers keep running after the app is killed, with snooze controls and notification actions.
- **Local-only, no auth** — all data stays on the device.
